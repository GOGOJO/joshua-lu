Happyend

October 2, 2026 · #movies

★★★★★ · Letterboxd →

Friendships, a topic I’ve been thinking a lot about recently. We all have conceptions of our friends and they have conceptions of us too. This conception we have of each other is comforting. But people change and we outgrow each other’s conceptions. When the understanding is shattered a crisis comes in. This change threatens the ego. You can see the ocean for the first time together, and spend your entire life together and still grow apart. It’s hard to let go of people because you share so many good memories together. Sometimes it ends with a loud bang, other times it ends with a quiet whimper. I know it sucks, but you have to ask yourself this important question. Would I be friends with this person if we met now? If the answer is yes then you should try to mend the misunderstanding. If the answer is no then you should let them go.

There is always a silver lining. With the extra space you invite new friendship, new experiences, and new love into your life.

Good film 🔥
