Personal website and personal growth

September 28, 2026

I’ve been procrastinating on making this personal website/blog for an embarrassingly long time, but the wait is over! I will be posting my journey in trying to land a big boy job in tech, my personal projects, hobbies, and my thoughts and feelings about anything that I am currently pondering.

I went to New York over the summer, and on the plane ride there, I remember that the flight attendant demonstrated that we should put on our own oxygen masks first before helping the children next to us. I feel like life is the same. I need to work on myself first before I can help the people I love and care about. I guess this personal website is a small first step in helping myself and becoming the person I want to become.
