Hedgehogs

September 28, 2026 · #poetry

I just learned what a ghazal is from my intro to poetry class and decided to try and write one. Inspo is the hedgehog’s dilemma from Neon Genesis Evangelion.

Ghazal, Hedgehogs

The closer we get the more we bleed
The more we draw for warmth the more we bleed

I reach for you and I mean no harm
Our quills stab each other and we both bleed

When I’m alone I yearn for connection
When I’m with someone I bleed

Before I speak I wonder how they will react
There’s a choice to freeze or to bleed

To live freely is to concede
To love is to bleed
